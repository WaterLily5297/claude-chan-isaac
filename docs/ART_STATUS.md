# Art inventory and work queue

Art inventory audited from source `8fd6a8c` on 2026-10-26, updated through the [fourth icon batch](ITEM_ART_BATCH_04.md) on 2026-10-27. The [92-icon registry](item-art-manifest.json) now records 40 designed and 52 placeholder icons. The four batches add offline icon review, not a new character or engine acceptance pass. The owner has not tested the installed `8fd6a8c`. Historical observations remain in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) and [POSE_REVIEW.md](POSE_REVIEW.md).

## Scope and evidence

There are **206 tracked art files: 181 PNGs and 25 ANM2 files**. The tables below enumerate every path once: 22 gameplay sheets, 22 stage/VS portraits, 22 dossier portraits, 22 dossier wrappers, 11 shared name strips, six menu files, 80 collectible icons, 12 trinket icons, one repository thumbnail and eight item-review PNGs. No font file is bundled. The five pills, Relay Slime and native effect/HUD presentation are listed separately because they do not add custom image files.

Counts, PNG headers, XML registrations and generator/manifests were inspected offline. Reported anatomy issues come from the earlier O5 review; the four item batches render and review their forty icons and original reference shapes. Geometry, exact regenerated bytes and provenance records alone do not establish aesthetic quality, licence clearance beyond the recorded terms, or in-game compatibility.
