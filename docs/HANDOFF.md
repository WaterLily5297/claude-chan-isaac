## CONSTITUTION-COOP-01: per-player item state (2026-10-24)

Source-only follow-up based directly on main `9805683`. The Constitution stored one pickup-heart bit and one floor-block bit for the whole run. Two-holder regressions fail on that baseline: the first holder consumes the other holder's reward and shield. Version 1 of `Risk.state.constitution` stores booleans by player slot instead. Tainted Lazarus uses separate body keys across Flip, including a Birthright-exposed twin, so returning to a previously active body cannot regrant its effects. Exit disables consumption; dead/ghost players cannot consume rewards. New floors reset shields only. Stat costs, excluded damage and the 60-frame refusal cooldown are unchanged.

Legacy saves cannot identify which player spent a shared effect, so migration copies existing consumed bits to players present at migration and removes the legacy fields. This avoids duplicate hearts or same-floor shields; subsequent floors and new players are independent. Existing once-per-run pickup behavior is retained, including drop/regain. The new fixture covers two holders, detached continue, floor reset, live replacement, migration and inactivity; ten injected faults fail. Full gate: 61 suites and 171 tracked Lua files. ## Vanilla compatibility pass (2026-10-24)

Source-only, offline; one small PR per fix, each with a regression that fails on the old code.

- **Fire-rate multipliers scale the whole shot interval.** Agentic, Cat-girl Mode and Rolled Back multiplied the raw `MaxFireDelay`, which slows the player down once Soy Milk, Almond Milk or stacked tears items push the delay below 0. New `scripts/fire_rate.lua` scales `(MaxFireDelay + 1)`, as the Qwen forms already did (they now share the helper). Cat-girl Mode is now an exact x2 fire rate. `tests/fire_rate_test.lua`.
- **Co-op ghosts cannot use DROP abilities.** A dead co-op player keeps its character type as a ghost, so its form's DROP ability (Releases, Slides, checks, Pounce...) stayed usable. `DropTap` now ignores `IsCoopGhost()` players, which covers every form at once. `tests/drop_tap_test.lua`.
- **The Constitution's refused hit now grants hurt invincibility (60 frames)**, like Holy Mantle; before, the same contact damage landed on the next frame and the block did nothing. Penalty-free damage (`DAMAGE_NO_PENALTIES`) no longer spends the block, matching Risk and Disgrace Loop. `tests/smoke_test.lua`.
- **Pizza Glue glazes the native tear color** instead of replacing it, so Ipecac, Spoon Bender and form tints stay readable. `tests/meme_items_test.lua`.
- **Run progression is protected from door and pickup items.** --dangerously-skip-permissions force-unlocked every locked door, including Mega Satan (key pieces), Boss Rush, the Blue Womb and the secret exits, and its rm -rf could delete the big chest, trophy or beds that end a run. All Tests Passing could mark an uncleared boss room clear (no trapdoor or chest: a stranded floor) and opened special exits. New `scripts/door_safety.lua` allows only doors into ordinary rooms of the floor (non-negative index, not secret rooms); new `scripts/pickup_safety.lua` protects run-ending pickups; All Tests Passing refuses, keeping its charge, in uncleared boss, Boss Rush and challenge rooms. EID, README and ITEMS.md updated. `tests/door_safety_test.lua`.
- **Co-op HUD lines no longer overlap.** Claude forms drew two lines in 24-pixel bands from y-40, DeepSeek forms one line per 12 pixels from y-40, and the nine roster families one line per 12 pixels from y-52, so mixed co-op (for example GPT as player 1 and DeepSeek as player 2, or Claude and DeepSeek) wrote over each other, and 3-4 players ran into the story captions. New `scripts/hud_layout.lua` gives every player one 24-pixel band (main line plus the line above it) and lifts story captions above the highest band; single-player positions are unchanged except roster forms moving 12 pixels down to the shared main row. Closes the older "HUD overlap in 3+ co-op" finding. `tests/hud_layout_test.lua`.
- **Run state is checkpointed every floor.** Mod state was saved only on a normal exit, so after a crash or forced close a continued run loaded state from an older session, or from a different run. Risk now also saves once per floor, on the first update after `POST_NEW_LEVEL` so every module's floor callbacks have already run. Engine check still needed: confirm the vanilla continue point after a crash matches the floor start. `tests/risk_checkpoint_test.lua`.
- **Vanilla characters play vanilla.** README documents the risk score as "Claude forms only", but `covered()` in risk.lua also included every vanilla (and other mods') character, so ordinary Isaac runs got the proxy IP, risk, up to x3 enemy HP, Hydra respawns, Relay Slimes and suspension. Coverage is now the normal and tainted Claude forms only; a vanilla co-op partner of Claude is never billed or suspended. The owner was offered an opt-in variant (joining the proxy through the 9.9 Yuan Relay); this change implements the documented behavior. `tests/risk_coverage_test.lua`, `tests/risk_damage_test.lua`.
- **Opus Mode bills every weapon.** It charged a coin per 10 tears only, so Brimstone, Technology, Mom's Knife, Dr./Epic Fetus and Ludovico kept x1.8 damage for free. It now bills laser starts, Dr. Fetus bombs and the shared paid-attack observer as well; child beams and placed bombs stay free. **Compact with Car Battery** no longer bills Claude's 50 usage twice. Engine checks for the whole pass are listed in TESTING.md (VANILLA-COMPAT-01). `tests/opus_wallet_test.lua`, `tests/smoke_test.lua`.
- **Co-op ghosts no longer take a share of blue flies.** Llama Releases and Open Weights counted every player with `IsDead() == false`, which includes co-op ghosts, so a ghost took two flies and the living player lost the solo share of four. New `Tools.Active` (target_tools.lua) excludes ghosts. Hardcoded Output, 529 Overloaded and Strawberry stay tear-only by design (documented). `tests/llama_release_test.lua`, `tests/backlog_more_test.lua`.
- **Horse Brb, Getting Food heals two hearts.** The pill ignored its color, so a horse pill (vanilla doubles every pill effect) healed the same one heart. It now heals two; the 3-second shooting lock is unchanged. EID and READMEs updated. `tests/meal_break_test.lua`.
- **Car Battery and Void with mod actives.** Ban Hammer's same-frame Car Battery use re-banned the enemies it had just killed, rolling every misban twice (double Risk); it now skips dead and charmed enemies. A slotless use (Void and similar report slot -1) no longer reaches `GetActiveItem(-1)`: Is This True? refunds nothing and the backlog charged-use watcher ignores it. `tests/smoke_test.lua`, `tests/backlog_items_test.lua`.
